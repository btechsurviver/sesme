About Our School

A Legacy of Excellence in Education

Founded in 2061 BS, SESAME International School has established itself as one of the premier educational institutions in Birgunj, Nepal. Our commitment to academic rigor, holistic development, and international standards sets us apart.

We believe every child possesses unique potential. Our student-centered approach nurtures curiosity, critical thinking, and creativity in a safe, inspiring environment that prepares students for the challenges and opportunities of tomorrow.

Campus

World-Class Facilities

Our campus is designed to inspire learning and foster growth, equipped with modern facilities that support academic, athletic, and creative development.

Modern Library & Resource Center

Our expansive library houses thousands of books, digital resources, and quiet study spaces. Students have access to a curated collection spanning academic references, literature, periodicals, and multimedia resources to support independent learning and research.

10,000+ Books Digital Resources Reading Zones

Sports & Athletic Complex

Physical development is integral to our educational philosophy. Our sports facilities include well-maintained grounds for football, cricket, basketball, and indoor games, supported by dedicated coaches who nurture athletic talent and sportsmanship.

Outdoor Grounds Indoor Games Expert Coaches

Smart Classrooms

Our technology-equipped classrooms create an interactive learning environment. Each room is designed with proper lighting, ventilation, and modern teaching aids to ensure an optimal learning experience for every student.

Interactive Learning Modern Equipment Optimal Design

Spacious Campus

Set in a lush, secure campus with well-maintained gardens and open spaces, our school provides a serene environment that promotes focus, creativity, and well-being. The campus includes dedicated areas for assembly, outdoor activities, and community gatherings.
